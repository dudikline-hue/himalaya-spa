### What was done to the source

The original is `generated_video.mp4`, 752×416, 10s, with an audio track.

- **Camera is genuinely locked off** — measured 0px of pan, which is why this
  beat every stock clip tried before it.
- **Made to loop by boomerang** (forward then reversed, dropping the duplicate
  turnaround frame). Verified lossless: the first and last frames are
  *identical*, seam exactly 0.000. Generated clips never loop on their own.
- **Upscaled to 1024×576** with lanczos and light denoise. The source is small,
  so it is encoded near its native size and left for the browser to scale;
  pushing it to 1280×720 cost megabytes for detail that was not there.
- Audio and the embedded cover-art stream were dropped.

### If you re-cut it

The wrap still measures ~2.9 against an average frame step of 0.6. That is
**not** a content jump — the two frames are the same picture. It is purely
H.264 drifting across the GOP on fine mist detail. Things that did not help:
a shorter GOP (40) made it worse and added a visible pulse every 1.6s; a
dissolve over the join changed nothing; higher resolution changed nothing.
Higher quality is the only lever, and it costs roughly a megabyte per step.

## Before you launch

Two things here are placeholders that should not go live as-is:

1. **`team-1/2/3.jpg` are stock portraits standing in for named practitioners.**
   Presenting stock models as your actual staff is misleading. Replace them with
   real photographs of real people, or drop the photos and keep the team section
   typographic.
2. **The founders on `about.html`** are invented, as are all treatment names,
   prices, statistics and testimonials. Swap in real content before publishing.
