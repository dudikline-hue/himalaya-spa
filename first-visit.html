How it goes

Four unhurried steps

Nothing is rushed and nothing is decided before you arrive. The plan is built with you, in the room.

Consultation

Twenty minutes, fully clothed, in a chair rather than on a bed. We talk through your history, your routine, and what is actually bothering you.

Skin reading

Magnified analysis and reference photography. We show you the screen — you should see what we see.

Your ritual

A treatment chosen after the reading, not before it. Usually a hydration or clarity protocol on a first visit; we save the advanced work for once we know your skin.

The plan

A written home routine and a suggested cadence. Emailed to you the same day, with no products you already own being replaced for the sake of it.

Before you come

A few small things

Arrive as you are

Makeup is fine — we remove it. Come ten minutes early if you would like tea first.

Pause retinoids

Skip prescription retinoids and acids for five days before an advanced treatment. Everything else can stay.

Bring your shelf

Photograph what you currently use, or bring the bottles. It makes the plan far more useful.

Questions we get

Straight answers

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, and it is free whether or not you book a treatment afterwards. You can come in, get a reading, take the plan and use it elsewhere. We would rather you did that than paid for something wrong.

Will I be sold products?

We will recommend what helps, name the price, and leave it there. Our practitioners are salaried, not commissioned.

How soon will I see a difference?

Hydration and tone shift the same day. Texture takes three to four sessions. Pigment and firmness are a six-month conversation. Anyone promising faster is selling something.

What is your cancellation policy?

Twenty-four hours' notice, no charge. Inside that window we ask for half the treatment cost, waived once per year for members.

Do you treat sensitive or reactive skin?

Often. Barrier repair is one of our specialities, and every protocol has a low-reactivity variant. Tell us at consultation and we will patch test.
