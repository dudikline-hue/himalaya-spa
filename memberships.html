Worth knowing

The fine print, in plain words

Can I share my membership?

The monthly treatment is yours, but guest rituals and retail discounts can be used by anyone you bring in.

What if I travel a lot?

Pause for up to three months a year at no cost. Visits banked before the pause stay banked.

Can I upgrade mid-month?

Yes, prorated to the day. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.
