About Himalaya

Science, softened

We opened Himalaya because skincare had become loud. We wanted somewhere quiet that still worked.

Our story

Built around the return visit

Most studios are designed for the first appointment — the one that sells. We designed ours for the twelfth.

That changes everything. Our rooms are quieter. Our protocols are gentler, because we are not trying to prove anything in a single session. Our aestheticians are salaried, not commissioned, so nobody has a reason to recommend a treatment you do not need.

What we do use is well-evidenced: peptides, retinoids, controlled resurfacing, radiofrequency. What we pair it with is botanical, cold-pressed, and formulated in small batches. Neither half works as well alone.

What we hold to

Four standards

Honest recommendations

If a treatment will not help you, we will say so. If a $30 product does the job of a $200 one, we will point at the $30 one.

No hidden pressure

No commission, no packages sold from the treatment bed, no upsell while you are lying down with your eyes closed.

Documented skin

We photograph and note every visit, so progress is something you can see rather than something we assert.

Time that is actually yours

A 75-minute ritual is 75 minutes of treatment. Consultation, changing, and aftercare sit outside the clock.
